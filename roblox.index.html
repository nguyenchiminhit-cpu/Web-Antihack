Built to catch the usual cheats.

The real work happens on the server. Lag protection keeps honest players with a bad connection safe from false flags.

Speed

Real movement is measured every quarter second. Cheaters are pulled back to their last safe position.

Jump and fly

Checks jump power, jump height, infinite jump and hanging in the air without moving.

Teleport

Large jumps in position at very high speed are detected and punished.

Hitbox

Body part sizes are remembered on spawn and restored if they change.

Aimbot

Shots that start too far away, pass through walls or snap between targets are rejected.

ESP

The client script reports highlights, name tags and boxes attached to other players.
